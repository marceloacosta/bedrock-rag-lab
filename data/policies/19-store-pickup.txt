Collecting an online order in a store

Store pickup has no delivery fee when the chosen store has the product available. Wait for the ready-for-collection message before travelling. An order confirmation is not a ready message. The store holds a ready order for five calendar days from the ready message. Contact the store if collection within that period is not possible; the published policy does not guarantee an extension.

Bring the collection reference and identification matching the collecting person registered on the order. A purchaser may nominate another adult in the order portal before collection. A forwarded email alone does not add that person to the collection record. Inspect large items at the store and ask staff for help with loading when needed.

Pickup eligibility and stock are specific to each store. The online product catalog does not establish which store has an item today. The published pickup process cannot answer a live stock question or reserve a product on its own. Product return and warranty windows start from the recorded collection date for a pickup order. Other eligibility conditions, including final-sale markings and product-specific trials, still apply.
