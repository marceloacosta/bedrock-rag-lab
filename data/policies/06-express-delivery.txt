Express order processing

Express delivery for eligible ordinary parcels costs USD 18. It has a two-business-day transit target after dispatch. Orders accepted before 13:00 Eastern Time on a business day are dispatched that day. Orders accepted at or after 13:00, or on a weekend, are dispatched on the next business day. Business days for this policy are Monday through Friday excluding published holidays.

The transit clock starts after dispatch, not when the customer adds an item to the basket. For an eligible order placed on a Monday at 14:00 during a week with no holidays, dispatch is Tuesday and the two-business-day transit target is Thursday. Payment review or an address problem can prevent dispatch until the issue is resolved. Do not infer that a particular order cleared those checks from this policy alone.

Some product categories cannot use express delivery. Check the lithium-delivery and oversize-delivery articles before promising this service. A customer cannot pay a surcharge to remove a product restriction. The membership benefit covers qualifying standard delivery only. It does not discount the USD 18 express price or change the cutoff time.
