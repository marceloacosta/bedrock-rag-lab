Swollen, leaking, or damaged rechargeable batteries

Stop using or charging a rechargeable device if its battery swells, leaks, or is visibly damaged. Do not send it through the ordinary returns process. The store's regular ground return label is not an approved way to ship a damaged battery. Contact the product safety team through the support form and choose the battery-safety category.

Provide the product model, purchase reference, a description of the damage, and photographs when it is safe to take them. The team gives the customer instructions for the specific product and location. Support agents must not improvise a packaging method or tell customers to remove a swollen battery. Follow the device manufacturer's safety instructions while waiting for the team.

The general shipping article permits certain undamaged battery products to travel by ground. That permission does not apply to a damaged battery. Whether the item qualifies for a warranty remedy is assessed separately from how it can be handled safely. This page does not publish compensation amounts, local disposal-site addresses, or a guaranteed replacement date. The safety process must be followed before any physical return is arranged.
