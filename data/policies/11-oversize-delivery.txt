Oversize delivery for kayaks and cargo boxes

Kayaks and rigid roof cargo boxes are oversize products. Delivery to an eligible address in the contiguous United States costs USD 35 per order containing oversize items. Neither the ordinary free-shipping threshold nor the membership threshold waives this fee. Adding a small accessory to the order does not turn the oversize shipment into an ordinary parcel.

Oversize deliveries use a scheduled ground carrier. Express parcel service is not available for these products. The carrier contacts the customer to arrange an appointment after the shipment is ready. The documentation does not provide a fixed appointment date for any particular order. Support must check the live shipment record before discussing an actual delivery appointment.

At delivery, inspect the outer packaging before signing and photograph visible transport damage. If damage is discovered afterward, use the delivery-damage reporting policy. Store pickup may be available for an oversize item when the chosen store has stock. A catalog listing alone does not establish that a particular store has it. Do not carry a damaged or unstable load away from a pickup location.
