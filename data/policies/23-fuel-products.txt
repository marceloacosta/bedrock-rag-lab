Camping fuel and stove returns

The online store sells empty camping stoves and accessories but does not ship filled fuel canisters. Fuel is sold for store collection only where a store is authorized to stock it. A stove listing does not mean fuel is included. Customers should check the compatible fuel type in the product instructions before buying fuel separately.

Never include a used or full fuel canister in a stove return. Remove any fuel container before arranging an eligible stove return and follow the manufacturer's instructions for making the stove safe. If the stove has leaked or appears unsafe, contact support for the product-specific procedure instead of using an ordinary label.

An unused stove bought online follows the standard return policy unless it was marked final sale. A stove bought as part of the starter kit uses the discounted-bundle allocation for its paid item value. The allocation does not authorize sending fuel through the mail. These pages do not list current store fuel stock, local collection hours, or the availability of a specific canister brand.
