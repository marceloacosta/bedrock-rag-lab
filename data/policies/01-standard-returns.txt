Standard returns for unused gear

The online store accepts ordinary unused gear within 30 calendar days after delivery. Customers must open a return request in the order portal before that window ends. After approval, they have seven calendar days to hand the parcel to the carrier. The request date determines whether the return was opened on time; the carrier acceptance date determines whether the shipping deadline was met.

Keep the original tags attached and include all parts. Trying a jacket on indoors does not count as using it. Washing a garment, taking a sleeping bag camping, or removing a safety seal does count as use. Used products cannot enter the ordinary change-of-mind return process. Hiking boots have a separate fit-trial policy, and damaged products have a separate warranty process. An exception for one product category does not extend the window for other products in the same order.

The warehouse checks the condition before approving the refund. A return request is not itself a refund. Customers receive a receipt after the inspection is complete. Original outbound delivery charges are not refunded for a change of mind. The return-label policy explains who pays for sending an item back. Do not send an item to the warehouse without an approved return request.
