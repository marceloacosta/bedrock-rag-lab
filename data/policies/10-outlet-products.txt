Outlet and final-sale products

Products marked final sale in the outlet cannot be returned merely because the customer changes their mind, chooses the wrong color, or finds a lower price later. The final-sale marking appears on the product page and the order confirmation. A general store promotion does not make a product final sale unless the product is specifically marked that way.

Final sale does not remove the manufacturing warranty. A defective outlet tent follows the tent warranty assessment process, including proof of purchase and photos. Cosmetic wear that was disclosed on the outlet listing is not a new manufacturing defect. Support must distinguish a disclosed condition from a fault that arose later.

If the delivered outlet product does not match the listing, report that problem within 14 calendar days after delivery. Keep photographs of the product and the packaging. The support team checks the listing and the received item before arranging a remedy. The 14-day mismatch report window is a separate route from the manufacturing warranty. Do not describe it as a 14-day change-of-mind return right. Stock for a replacement outlet product is not guaranteed.
