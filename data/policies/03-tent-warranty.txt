Tent manufacturing warranty

Tents have a 24-month warranty for manufacturing defects, beginning on the purchase date. A stitching seam that separates during normal use can qualify. The customer must provide proof of purchase and clear photos so the repair team can assess the failure. The warranty team chooses repair or replacement after assessment; support cannot promise a cash refund before that decision.

Damage from animals, sharp objects, storms beyond the tent's rating, ordinary fabric wear, or incorrect storage is outside this warranty. Accidental damage can still be sent for a paid repair assessment. A quotation must be approved before paid repair work starts. The repair-price guide lists the fixed fees that are currently published; some repairs require an individual quote.

Customers should dry a tent before packing it for inspection. Do not include fuel canisters, batteries, food, or personal belongings. An approved manufacturing-defect claim receives a prepaid ground label. For a paid repair, shipping is included only if the individual quotation says so. Warranty decisions apply to the specific damage inspected. A previous approved claim does not guarantee that a different problem will also be covered.
