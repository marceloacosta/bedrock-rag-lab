Standard delivery prices

For ordinary parcels to the contiguous United States, standard ground delivery costs USD 6 when the qualifying merchandise subtotal is below USD 75. At USD 75 or more, standard delivery is free. The qualifying subtotal is calculated after discounts and before sales tax. Gift cards do not count toward it. A basket that was above the threshold before a discount may fall below it afterward.

An active store membership reduces the free standard-delivery threshold to USD 40. The membership must be active when the order is placed. Becoming a member later does not retroactively refund an earlier delivery charge. Membership is linked to the purchasing customer, not automatically to everyone at the same address.

Oversize products have a separate delivery charge and do not receive free delivery under either threshold. The oversize-delivery article explains the fee. Express service has its own price and restrictions; free standard delivery does not mean free express delivery. Any original outbound shipping charge remains separate from the item price when the customer later makes a change-of-mind return. The checkout presents the final delivery options before payment.
