Delivery restrictions for rechargeable products

Products containing lithium batteries travel by ground service within the contiguous United States. The rule covers rechargeable lanterns, headlamps, power banks, and navigation devices, including products whose batteries are installed inside the equipment. These products cannot be sent by our air services, to Alaska or Hawaii, or to an international destination.

Selecting express shipping at checkout does not override this restriction. If a mixed order includes an ordinary item and a rechargeable device, the rechargeable item still travels by ground. A support agent must not promise that paying the express surcharge makes a battery shipment eligible for air transport. The customer can remove the battery product and place a separate order for the other items if those items qualify for the requested service.

Ground shipping has the delivery estimate shown at checkout for the destination. This policy does not supply a guaranteed arrival date for a particular parcel. Tracking events come from the carrier after dispatch. Return transport for a device with a swollen, leaking, or damaged battery follows the damaged-battery procedure rather than the ordinary parcel-return process. Customers must not mail such a battery using an ordinary return label.
