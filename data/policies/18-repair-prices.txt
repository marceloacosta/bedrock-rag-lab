Published repair charges

The repair workshop offers a tent pole-section replacement for USD 18 per section and a sleeping-bag zipper-slider replacement for USD 25. These prices cover the named repair work only. Any transport charge is stated in the individual quotation. Other repairs, including a torn tent floor, require a quote after assessment. There is no published flat price for a tent-floor replacement.

A manufacturing fault approved under warranty is handled under the product's warranty terms rather than automatically charged as a paid repair. An accidental tear or wear outside the warranty can still be assessed for paid repair. The workshop tells the customer the scope and price before work begins. The customer must approve the quotation; sending an item for assessment is not approval for an unspecified charge.

Active members receive priority queue handling but pay the same published repair prices. Priority is not a completion guarantee. Current workshop capacity, parts availability, and a particular item's repair status are maintained in the live repair system, not in this price list. A request for a completion date requires checking that record. Do not infer it from a price or from membership alone.
