Reporting delivery damage or the wrong product

Report visible transport damage or a wrong product within seven calendar days after delivery. Keep the outer packaging and photograph the label, packing materials, and affected item. The support team compares those details with the shipment record before approving the claim. If the item is unsafe to use, stop using it while the report is assessed.

An approved wrong-product or delivery-damage return receives a prepaid label. The customer does not pay the ordinary USD 7 change-of-mind label fee for that approved return. The team confirms whether a replacement or another remedy will be provided. A report being opened does not prove replacement inventory exists or establish a delivery date for the replacement.

Damage involving a rechargeable battery follows the separate damaged-battery procedure. Do not put a leaking or swollen battery in an ordinary return parcel even when support is investigating delivery damage. A manufacturing defect discovered later follows the product's warranty process. The seven-day transport-report window does not shorten a separately applicable manufacturing warranty.
