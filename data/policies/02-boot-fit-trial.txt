Hiking boot fit trial

Hiking boots bought from the online store have a 60-calendar-day fit trial, measured from delivery. This includes boots worn on ordinary outdoor walks. The purpose is to let customers discover pressure points that may not appear during an indoor fitting. The customer can claim one fit-trial return in any rolling 12-month period. Record the previous claim date before approving another pair.

The trial excludes damage from misuse, missing parts, and boots altered by a third party. Normal trail dust and wear from walking do not by themselves disqualify a pair. Remove loose dirt before packing. The boots must be returned as a complete pair. Boots that satisfy the trial receive a refund of the item price paid; original delivery charges remain nonrefundable.

Start the request in the normal returns portal and select the fit-trial reason. The standard seven-day dispatch deadline after approval still applies. This trial changes the eligibility window and acceptable wear for hiking boots only. It does not change the price of a return label. Read the return-label policy for the applicable fee and the membership policy for any shipping benefits. The fit trial does not promise a particular replacement model will be in stock.
