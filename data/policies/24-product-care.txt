Drying and storing outdoor equipment

Dry tents completely before putting them into long-term storage. Moisture trapped in folded fabric can cause mold, odors, and damage to coatings. Brush off loose dirt gently and follow the product's cleaning instructions. Do not use a washing machine or household bleach on a tent. A storage problem is not automatically a manufacturing defect.

Keep sleeping bags dry and store them loosely when space allows. Long-term compression can reduce loft. The product label controls the permitted washing method; different insulation and fabrics may need different treatment. A support agent should not infer washing-machine compatibility merely from the generic sleeping-bag category.

Rechargeable equipment should follow its manufacturer's charging and storage instructions. If a battery is swollen, leaking, or damaged, use the battery-safety process. Generic care advice does not authorize continued charging. This article does not supply a temperature specification for every model, a chemical treatment recipe, or a prediction of remaining battery life. Refer product-specific questions to the relevant manual or product team when the required detail is absent.
