Who pays for a return label

For ordinary domestic change-of-mind returns, the store offers a ground label for USD 7 per parcel. If the customer uses it, USD 7 is deducted from the refund or store credit. A customer may instead arrange and pay for their own suitable carrier. A store label covers one parcel; two parcels require two labels. Membership does not waive this fee.

An approved wrong-item shipment or manufacturing-defect claim receives a prepaid label without the USD 7 deduction. The support team must approve that reason first. A customer selecting a defect reason in the portal is not sufficient proof that the claim has been approved. The hiking boot fit trial is a fit return, so the normal USD 7 label fee applies unless the customer supplies their own shipping.

Use suitable packaging and keep the carrier acceptance receipt. Items containing an undamaged lithium battery must use the eligible ground service. A damaged, swollen, or leaking battery must never be sent using this ordinary label; use the damaged-battery procedure. Label creation does not count as dispatch. The parcel must actually be handed to the carrier within the return's dispatch deadline.
