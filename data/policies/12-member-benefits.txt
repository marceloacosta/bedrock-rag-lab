Store membership benefits

An active annual membership costs USD 24 and lasts 12 months from purchase. Members receive the lower standard-delivery threshold described in the shipping-price policy and can join the equipment-repair queue with priority handling. Priority handling changes the queue position; it does not make the repair free or guarantee a completion date.

Membership does not waive return-label charges, express-delivery charges, oversize-delivery charges, or repair prices. It does not extend the standard return window or the boot fit-trial window. A customer who has both a membership and an eligible product-specific benefit can use those benefits under their separate rules. Support should state which benefit applies rather than calling all shipping or returns free.

Benefits start when membership is activated. Orders placed before activation retain their original delivery charges. A membership is attached to one customer account and cannot be shared across unrelated accounts. The policy explains the benefits and annual duration; it does not reveal a particular customer's current membership status. Check the customer's membership record before applying benefits to a live order. Refund treatment for an already-used membership is not specified in this policy.
