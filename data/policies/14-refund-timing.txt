When refunds arrive

The returns team normally inspects an ordinary parcel within five business days after warehouse receipt. Once the return is approved, the store submits the refund within two business days. A bank-card provider may then take another three to five business days to show the credit. These stages happen in sequence. A carrier delivery notification is not the same event as a completed inspection.

For planning, the longest published path is five business days for inspection, two for submission, and five for the card provider: up to 12 business days after warehouse receipt. That is a policy estimate, not a live payment-status lookup. Weekends and published holidays do not count as business days. A missing component or a dispute over condition can require further review.

Gift recipients receiving store credit are sent that credit after approval. The three-to-five-day bank-card processing period does not apply to issuing store credit. This page does not identify whether a particular return has been received, approved, or refunded. For that, support needs the live return record. A screenshot of the original purchase receipt does not show the refund's current status.
