Price adjustments after a purchase

If the store lowers the price of the identical model, size, and color within seven calendar days of an online purchase, the purchaser may request one price adjustment. The lower-priced item must be available to buy when the request is made. A price on another retailer's website does not qualify. A different color or size is not an identical item for this policy.

Final-sale outlet items, bundle promotions, gift-card purchases, and one-use personalized coupons are excluded. The adjustment returns the eligible price difference to the original payment method after approval. It does not change the original order date, extend the return window, or remove a product's shipping restrictions.

Support needs the original purchase record and the current matching listing to decide a live request. These policy documents contain the rule, not today's product prices or inventory. A customer asking for the exact difference on a particular order needs those live values. An ordinary exchange for the same model in a different size follows the exchange-price rule instead. Do not treat a size exchange as a second price adjustment.
