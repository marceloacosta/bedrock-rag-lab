International deliveries and returns

The store ships eligible ordinary products to Canada and the United Kingdom. International standard shipping costs USD 22 per order. Domestic membership and ordinary free-delivery thresholds do not apply to international shipping. Local import taxes and customs charges are paid by the recipient and are separate from the amount charged by the store.

Lithium-battery products, kayaks, and rigid roof cargo boxes are excluded from international delivery. An item being small enough to fit in a parcel does not remove a lithium-battery restriction. An international customer must not select a domestic destination as a placeholder and expect support to reroute a restricted product across the border.

Eligible international change-of-mind returns use the normal unused-condition and return-window rules. The customer arranges and pays the return transport. The store does not issue the USD 7 domestic return label for an international parcel. Import taxes are not refunded by the store. Direct size or color exchanges are unavailable for international orders; return an eligible item and place a new order instead. Availability, exchange rates, and customs clearance times for a particular order are not maintained in these policy pages.
